Morning arrived quietly, but neither of them seemed particularly willing to acknowledge it.

Dhruv opened his eyes slowly, consciousness returning in reluctant pieces. His mind made a brief, responsible attempt at getting up before his body firmly rejected the idea.

The room was cool, the blanket thick and warm, and somewhere during the night his wife had apparently decided that a normal sleeping position was far too inefficient.

So now she was attached to him.

One arm locked around his waist. One leg thrown over him with surprising confidence for someone barely half his size.

A highly coordinated operation designed specifically to prevent him from leaving the bed.

Dhruv stared at the ceiling for a moment before a small smile appeared on his face.

Then he turned his head toward her.

Riya was buried against his shoulder, completely unguarded in sleep. A tiny thing, despite her constant insistence that she was "perfectly healthy-sized," and somehow the amount of space she occupied on the bed still made absolutely no sense.

Not that she would ever admit that.

Her face in sleep was a different thing from her face awake — no performance in it, no mischief loading behind the eyes, no expression preparing itself for deployment. Just her. Breathing slow and even, lips slightly parted.

There was hair in her mouth.

A strand of it, long, had found its way past her lips sometime in the night and she was — he looked closer — she was, in fact, chewing it. Slightly. With the focused commitment of someone who had identified a resource and was making use of it.

He considered this for a moment. Sighed softly through a smile.

Carefully, he pulled the strands free and brushed her hair away from her face. His palm settled gently against her cheek, the soft skin flattening slightly beneath his hand.

"Riya," he called quietly.

Warmly.

Like he wasn't entirely sure whether he wanted her to wake up or not.

No response.

Not even a twitch.

His thumb brushed slowly against her cheek again before he leaned down and pressed a soft kiss to her forehead.

Nothing.

Then her cheek. And stayed there a little longer.

Still nothing.

Dhruv narrowed his eyes slightly.

Interesting.

He leaned closer this time, slow enough for anticipation to build.

She lasted approximately two seconds.

The giggle came from somewhere she hadn't managed to suppress in time — bright and involuntary, entirely betraying her — but the kiss never came.

Instead, his hands landed on her waist.

"Dhruv—!"

The rest dissolved into laughter as he started tickling her mercilessly.

Riya twisted violently beneath him, kicking against the blanket and trying to pry his hands away while laughing so hard she could barely breathe.

"No— stop— stop! I'm awake! I'm awake!"

Dhruv, unfortunately for her, showed no intention of mercy.

Eventually she managed to escape, scrambling dramatically to the other side of the bed while clutching the blanket to herself like she had barely survived a life-threatening attack.

Dhruv laughed quietly at the sight.

Riya breathing heavily puffed her cheeks immediately.

"Now it's my turn."

Before he could respond, she launched herself at him.

Dhruv barely had enough time to brace before she climbed onto him triumphantly, trapping his waist between her legs as her hands attacked his stomach in retaliation.

The effect was… minimal.

His stomach barely reacted beneath her tickling, which only made Riya more determined.

He exhaled. Sharply. Shifted. Let his hands fall open.

She sat back — triumphant, radiantly so — watching him raise one hand to cover his face in defeat.

Riya fell backward onto the bed beside him, giggling in victory while Dhruv covered his face with one hand, pretending to mourn his loss when he was really hiding another laugh.

The room slowly settled again, their laughter fading into the soft hum of the fan overhead.

After a moment, Dhruv pushed himself upright and sat quietly at the edge of the bed.

Almost immediately, Riya moved toward him again.

She wrapped her arms around him from behind, pressing herself against his back with sleepy satisfaction before placing a soft kiss against his cheek.

Dhruv's hands rested over hers instinctively.

And for a few seconds, neither of them moved.

Then, gently, he loosened her hold and stood up despite her quiet protest.

Riya remained on the bed, sitting on her knees beneath the tangled blanket while watching him with heavy morning eyes.

Dhruv walked to the window and pulled the curtains apart.

Light came in all at once — eager, unhesitating — filling the room the way light fills rooms when it has been waiting just outside and someone finally lets it in. It landed on everything. The blanket. The scattered pillows. The small figure still sitting on the bed, legs folded beneath her, blinking.

He looked back at her.

Good morning, he didn't say.

He didn't need to.
