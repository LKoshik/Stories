He started brushing his teeth, watching his girl throwing a tantrum on the bed — face moving left and right, buried into the pillow, hands and legs flapping.

"I don't want to go to work."

He rinsed before the paste could get in, holding his laughter. "Ya ya, stay like that. Your boss is going to come pick you up personally."

"Mmhhh."

He put his brush in their lovely holder — pink, with a heart design on it. Took his towel from the wardrobe.

"I'm going in, Riya. Don't break the door when you see the clock."

She immediately looked up. Her hands ran toward her phone.

"It's 7 o'clock already!!"

She jumped out of bed, rushing toward the bathroom — but a wall twice her size was blocking the entrance.

"Mooove, I'm going to be late."

Her face pushed into his stomach, hands working past his waist, legs doing their best at moonwalking.

He tried his best to hold it in.

The moonwalk got him.

"What are you doing?" he said, laughing softly.

She pushed him into the bathroom. "Hmph."

Grabbed her toothbrush and started brushing aggressively.

"Be careful not to dissolve the teeth."

"Aarrghhh—" She continued brushing. "EEEE." "AAAA." Posing in front of the mirror.

He stood there a moment, towel on his shoulder, hands on his waist.

Giggling.

Then a long breath out.

"Haaaa."

He closed the curtain and started bathing.

— — —

She finished, went out, opened her wardrobe, picked a bunch of outfits, and spread them on the bed. By the time she'd narrowed it down to three, his bath was over. She gave a quick glance at him and then at the outfits.

"I know," she said, grabbed her towel, and walked toward the bathroom.

He turned on the fan and sat on the bed drying his hair.

"Dhruv, can you please pick one from the three? I'll be back."

"Huh?"

She closed the door before he could say anything else.

"Thank you," came through the closed door — from someone who had never once offered no as an option.

He heard it and gave a brief smile, shaking his head.

He picked each one up, flipped it front and back, tried to imagine how she'd look in it.

Wait a minute.

He looked again.

Blue kurti.

Blue kurti.

Green kurti.

"Riya. Aren't these two blue kurtis the same?"

The shower went quiet.

Silence.

"Ah — I mean — yeah, both look beautiful in different ways."

His eyes went back and forth between them. He still couldn't tell the difference.

They are the same, he decided confidently, and set one aside.

He started getting ready. The door opened slowly — his girl standing there in a towel with damp hair, hands on her waist, fists closed. The look of someone determined to educate a particular someone who had clearly grown up with no women around.

"I picked this one," he said, adjusting his tie. "It'll look great on you."

She looked at the clock. She looked at what he'd picked. The determination softened slightly.

"Nice pick," she said.

He turned away.

Okay, she liked it 🤷🏻‍♂️.

She tapped his shoulder and held it up quickly.

"See — the neck design. The one you picked has a mandarin collar and embroidery, the other one has a V-neck and a different pattern. Got it?"

His eyes widened. Mouth slightly open.

"Oh — oh, I knew that. I was just playing around."

She stared at him.

Silence.

Dress hanging in her hand.

A smile cracked through his poker face.

"Hmph."

She turned her back to him, hiding a smile that didn't want to be caught.

— — —

7:40. She was supposed to be in office by 8.

"I'm ready" — which was what she said every time, while still applying something till the last possible second.

He went down and brought the car to the gate. She locked up and settled inside in a hurry.

And then the day started.
